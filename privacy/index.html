HeartDrive — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

HeartDrive (“the app”) holds your heart rate at a target by adjusting your smart trainer’s resistance while you ride. This policy explains exactly what data the app uses and what happens to it. The short version: your data stays on your devices, and we do not have a server, collect it, sell it, or use it for advertising.

What the app accesses

  • Heart rate, read through Apple HealthKit during a workout on your Apple Watch (or a paired heart-rate source).
  • Live cycling metrics (power, cadence, speed) read directly from your Bluetooth smart trainer.

How it’s used

  • Your heart rate drives a control loop that sets your trainer’s target power in real time, so the app can hold your chosen heart rate.
  • It’s shown to you on the app’s dashboard and watch screen.
  • If you turn on “Broadcast HR to Zwift,” the app re-transmits your live heart rate over local Bluetooth as a standard heart-rate sensor, so an app you choose (such as Zwift, on a nearby device) can display it. This is a direct device-to-device Bluetooth link that you control; it is not sent to us or to any server.

Where your data goes

  • All processing happens on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Your workout and heart-rate samples are stored by Apple Health on your own devices, under your control.
  • HeartDrive has no servers and no accounts. It does not upload your health, fitness, or personal information to the developer or to any third party.
  • There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Sharing and selling

We never sell your data, never share it for advertising or marketing, and never use it for data mining. The only transmission of your heart rate is the optional, local Bluetooth broadcast described above, which goes only to the fitness app you choose to pair with.

Your control

  • You can revoke the app’s access to your health data at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › HeartDrive.
  • Because we store nothing on our side, removing the app and revoking Health access leaves no data with us.

Children

HeartDrive is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
